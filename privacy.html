Local-First Privacy for AI-Native CAE面向 AI 原生 CAE 的本地优先隐私政策

This policy explains how VibeFlow handles project files, prompt context, local diagnostics, third-party model services, and website links. It is written for users who run engineering workflows where source code, simulation inputs, solver logs, screenshots, results, and reports may contain valuable or confidential information. 本政策说明 VibeFlow 如何处理项目文件、Prompt 上下文、本地诊断信息、第三方模型服务和网站外链。它面向真实工程工作流用户，因为源码、仿真输入、求解日志、截图、结果和报告都可能包含有价值或保密的信息。

1. Scope1. 适用范围

This Privacy Policy applies to VibeFlow Studio, the VibeFlow website, documentation links, community entry points, and related AI-native CAE workflows operated or published by VibeFlow. 本隐私政策适用于 VibeFlow Studio、VibeFlow 网站、文档入口、社区入口，以及由 VibeFlow 提供或发布的 AI 原生 CAE 相关工作流。

VibeFlow is designed as a local-first engineering workbench. It does not require you to upload complete CAE projects to a VibeFlow-hosted cloud service in order to create geometry, generate meshes, run solvers, post-process results, or write reports. VibeFlow 被设计为本地优先的工程工作台。创建几何、生成网格、运行求解器、后处理结果或撰写报告时，并不要求您把完整 CAE 项目上传到 VibeFlow 托管的云服务。

This policy describes current product behavior and general privacy practices. It is not legal advice. If your organization has contractual, export-control, industry-specific, or regulatory requirements, review the full deployment environment before using external model services. 本政策描述当前产品行为和一般隐私实践，不构成法律意见。如果您的组织存在合同、出口管制、行业规范或监管要求，请在使用外部模型服务前审查完整部署环境。

2. Local-First Design2. 本地优先设计

Your project workspace is the primary storage location. Geometry scripts, CAD exports, mesh files, solver dictionaries or input decks, runtime logs, post-processing scripts, screenshots, Markdown notes, LaTeX files, PDFs, and reports are stored in the directories you choose on your machine. 您的项目工作区是主要存储位置。几何脚本、CAD 导出、网格文件、求解器字典或输入文件、运行日志、后处理脚本、截图、Markdown 笔记、LaTeX 文件、PDF 和报告都会存储在您机器上选择的目录中。

Local files may be read by VibeFlow components to rebuild the workflow, open previews, generate solver inputs, diagnose errors, or resume work. Local processing does not, by itself, mean that a file has been sent to a remote AI model or third-party platform. VibeFlow 组件可能读取本地文件，用于重建工作流、打开预览、生成求解器输入、诊断错误或继续任务。本地处理本身并不意味着文件已经发送给远程 AI 模型或第三方平台。

4. AI Model Context4. AI 模型上下文

When you use an AI agent, the model may receive the instruction and context you choose to provide. This can include selected files, highlighted code, solver logs, case parameters, screenshots, Markdown notes, LaTeX fragments, or other materials you add to chat. 当您使用 AI Agent 时，模型可能接收您选择提供的指令和上下文。这可能包括您加入对话的选中文件、代码片段、求解日志、算例参数、截图、Markdown 笔记、LaTeX 片段或其他材料。

You should not add confidential, personal, export-controlled, or proprietary engineering information to a prompt unless you intend to share that context with the selected model endpoint and your organization permits doing so. 除非您明确希望将相关上下文发送给所选模型端点，并且您的组织允许这样做，否则请不要把保密、个人、出口管制或专有工程信息加入 Prompt。

No hidden full-project upload不进行隐藏式完整项目上传

VibeFlow's intended workflow is explicit context sharing: the useful snippets, files, screenshots, or structured parameters are selected for the task. The fact that a file exists in your local workspace does not mean that the complete project is automatically sent to a model provider. VibeFlow 的预期工作方式是显式共享上下文：根据任务选择有用的片段、文件、截图或结构化参数。某个文件存在于本地工作区，并不表示完整项目会被自动发送给模型提供商。

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services5. 共享与第三方服务

VibeFlow can work with tools and model endpoints such as Codex, Claude Code, opencode, CC-Switch, domestic models, overseas model APIs, and self-hosted or enterprise deployments. Data handling by those services is governed by the provider, endpoint, workspace, and account configuration you choose. VibeFlow 可配合 Codex、Claude Code、opencode、CC-Switch、国内模型、海外模型 API，以及自托管或企业部署使用。这些服务如何处理数据，取决于您选择的提供商、端点、工作区和账号配置。

The website may link to documentation, GitHub releases, Bilibili videos, email, and community QR codes. Visiting those platforms may involve their own cookies, analytics, account systems, and privacy terms. VibeFlow does not control those third-party platforms. 本网站可能链接到使用文档、GitHub 发布页、Bilibili 视频、邮箱和社区二维码。访问这些平台时，可能涉及其自身的 cookie、统计、账号系统和隐私条款。VibeFlow 不控制这些第三方平台。

6. Retention and Deletion6. 保留与删除

Local project files and logs remain in your workspace until you delete, archive, or move them. VibeFlow may create local artifacts so that a workflow can be reproduced, audited, or resumed. 本地项目文件和日志会保留在您的工作区中，直到您删除、归档或移动它们。VibeFlow 可能创建本地产物，以便工作流能够复现、审计或继续执行。

If you send prompt context to a third-party model service, retention and deletion of that remote context depend on the selected provider's policy and your account settings. For sensitive work, consider offline workflows, local models, self-hosted endpoints, enterprise accounts, or stricter prompt-context review. 如果您将 Prompt 上下文发送给第三方模型服务，远程上下文的保留和删除取决于所选提供商的政策与您的账号设置。对于敏感工作，可考虑离线工作流、本地模型、自托管端点、企业账号或更严格的 Prompt 上下文审查。

7. Security7. 安全措施

VibeFlow reduces exposure by keeping engineering artifacts local by default and by making AI context selection part of the workflow. You are responsible for securing your operating system, project directory, credentials, API keys, model-provider accounts, and network environment. VibeFlow 通过默认将工程产物保留在本地，并把 AI 上下文选择作为工作流的一部分，来降低暴露面。您需要负责保护操作系统、项目目录、凭据、API Key、模型服务账号和网络环境。

  • Do not place API keys, passwords, or private tokens in prompts unless strictly necessary.除非绝对必要，不要把 API Key、密码或私有 Token 放入 Prompt。
  • Review screenshots before using Save and add to chat, especially when CAD metadata, file paths, or private results are visible.使用 Save and add to chat 前请检查截图，尤其是其中可见 CAD 元数据、文件路径或私有结果时。
  • Use separate workspaces or access controls for confidential client, defense, medical, energy, or regulated engineering projects.对于客户保密、国防、医疗、能源或受监管工程项目，请使用独立工作区或访问控制。

8. Your Choices and Rights8. 您的选择与权利

You control which local files, snippets, screenshots, parameters, and logs are added to chat. You can keep sensitive work offline, use a local or enterprise model endpoint, remove local logs, or choose not to use external model APIs for a particular project. 您可以控制哪些本地文件、片段、截图、参数和日志被加入对话。您可以让敏感工作保持离线，使用本地或企业模型端点，删除本地日志，或在特定项目中不使用外部模型 API。

Where applicable law grants privacy rights, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent for personal information that VibeFlow or a service provider processes. The exact rights and process depend on your location, the service used, and the relevant provider. 在适用法律赋予隐私权利的情况下，您可能有权请求访问、更正、删除、限制处理、反对处理、数据可携带，或撤回对个人信息处理的同意。具体权利和流程取决于您的所在地、所使用的服务以及相关提供商。

VibeFlow is not intended for children and is designed for engineering, research, and professional simulation work. VibeFlow 并非面向儿童设计，而是面向工程、研究和专业仿真工作。

We may update this policy as VibeFlow evolves, especially when model integrations, deployment modes, diagnostics, website services, or community channels change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest update. 随着 VibeFlow 演进，我们可能更新本政策，尤其是在模型集成、部署模式、诊断方式、网站服务或社区渠道发生变化时。页面顶部日期表示最近一次更新。
